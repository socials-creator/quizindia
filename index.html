    How to play

    Point to the region named in the challenge card. Choose States & UTs for all 28 States and 8 Union Territories, Districts to point out the districts of a single state, Airports to locate India’s international and major domestic airports (tap within about 60 km of the airport), or Outlines to recognise a state or UT from its outline alone and type its 2-letter licence plate code (it submits after 2 letters), Capitals to tap the capital cities, Nature to find rivers, mountains, parks and lakes, or Learn to study a state before you quiz. States and UTs are shown with their code in brackets, e.g. Karnataka (KA). You can zoom and pan the map to reach small or distant regions.

    Wrong taps and skips count as misses. The round ends when you find every region or reach five misses.

    What’s included

    • 28 States of India
    • 8 Union Territories
    • One quiz with every State and Union Territory
    • Districts mode: all 28 States — Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh (55), Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Punjab, Rajasthan (41), Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh (75), Uttarakhand and West Bengal — plus one choice for all the Union Territories (incl. Delhi’s 13 districts, J&K’s 22 (incl. Muzaffarabad and Mirpur, PoJK), Ladakh’s 8 and Lakshadweep’s 10 sub-divisions)
    • Airports mode: 111 airports (international plus major domestic), with a 60 km tap radius (never less than 35 km; 35–50 km where airports are close together) around each
    • Outlines mode: guess all 36 states and UTs from their outlines by typing the licence plate code (e.g. TN, MH)
    • Capitals mode: 38 capital cities — every state and Union Territory (Chandigarh serves Punjab, Haryana and the UT of Chandigarh) plus the second or seasonal capitals: Jammu, Dharamshala, Bhararisain (Gairsain) and Nagpur. The question shows only the city; the state appears after you answer
    • Nature mode: 92 features — 30 rivers, 6 mountain ranges, 10 major peaks and the highest peak of 25 states/UTs (where a verified highest point is available), 14 national parks, 9 lakes and 2 deserts/salt flats; pick a category or take them all
    • Learn mode: a study hub with everything from the other modes — States & UTs (facts, outline, registration code), Districts (names and flashcards), Capitals, Airports and Nature — with maps, search, and links between a state and its capital, airports, rivers, peaks, parks and lakes
    • Review mistakes: every wrong answer is remembered per mode, and a button starts a round of just those
    • Recent games, personal bests and your accuracy saved on this device

    Map source & reference

    Clickable boundaries come from the open india-maps-data GeoJSON/TopoJSON collection. Official names and the national political-map reference follow Survey of India and Government of India sources.

    Map note

    This is an educational pointing game, not a legal or survey-grade boundary product. District boundaries and names may not reflect the very latest reorganisations. Tap the status chip at the top (“Online · save offline”) once to download the app and every map (about 26 MB) so it works without internet; this needs https, such as GitHub Pages. River lines are simplified Natural Earth data; mountain crest lines and lake outlines are approximate.
